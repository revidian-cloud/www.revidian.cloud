Hey folks.
I'm back with a short one.
I've hit a wall of which I am trying to push my way through.
It's very frustrating.

<!--more-->

I need to get people's attention onto Melitix and then get their feedback.
**I am failing to do this.**
Let's walk through this as if it were an attention funnel, starting from the top/largest group of users.

1. All potential users (the market) \- this is all people who organize or attend meet-ups. Before COVID-19, this was a HUGE number.  
1. People upset with Meetup.com \- this is also a large number. I don't have an actual number for this but many, many people, like me, are upset with the status quo and want something better. If you look on Twitter, Reddit, and even their app's Google Play reviews, you'll see comments from upset people. Mastodon's Andy Piper recently blogged about his disappointment on this topic: [Meetup.com is so over](https://andypiper.co.uk/2024/10/18/meetup-com-is-so-over/).  
1. People looking for an alternative \- regardless of how unhappy some Meetup.com users are, some will stay. Others will simply stop being involved with meet-ups period. This lowers the number of users a good amount from the level above.  
1. People willing to try Melitix  
1. People willing to try out Melitix with their own in-person events

Those last two levels are what I'm stuck on.
I can't find people who want to test the alpha of Melitix.
I can't find meet-up organizers to speak with to pitch Melitix.


## Lack of people willing to try Melitix

For level 4, part of the problem is spam.
The majority of places where I predict meet-up organizers might hang out, I don't know how to reach them without being called out for spam and being blocked.
I've been banned from subreddits before for trying to ask people about one of my projects.

I did have a couple of small conversations, but nothing substantial.
Even in Slack communities where I planned to reach out, I've now seen a notice that if you solicit via DMs you will be banned.
:anguish: **What is a solopreneur to do?**


## In-person events

My Melitix thesis is that we need an event platform with better technology, a better business model, and some form of federation.
The direction I'm going regarding product decisions is all based on the value of in-person meet-ups.
Melitix supports online meet-ups, but the intent was to make that the backup solution, not the norm.

The community research I've done recently suggests that not only are many meet-ups online right now but there's a shift moving even more towards that direction.
This reality doesn't gel well with the current product direction of Melitix.

I'm trying to determine if, in today's day and age, online meet-ups are genuinely what people prefer.
An alternate theory is after covid, finding venues for meet-ups is still tricky, and if this became easier, many groups would move back to in-person events.
To answer this question, we return to my original problem in this blog post.
**I can't find people, particularly event organizers in this case, to speak with.**

Do you have any ideas? Please [reach out](https://www.feliciano.tech/).
